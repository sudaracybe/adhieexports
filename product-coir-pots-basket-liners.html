Coir Pots & Basket Liners

Biodegradable coir pots and hanging-basket liners for shock-free transplanting

Coir Pots & Basket Liners

Adhie Coir Pots are made from coconut fibre from the outer layer of the husk, bonded with natural latex. Fully biodegradable, they are widely used in horticulture, ornamental, vine and tree nurseries and in domestic gardening. Their high permeability to water, air and roots gives faster cultivation, an excellent root system and re-establishment without transplant shock, even in cold, drought or adverse seasons. Pots come in different sizes, for manual or automated soil filling, and pair well with our Coco Discs.

Adhie Coir Basket Liners come in rubberized and needled coir types. They give excellent drainage and aeration, preventing root rot, and are a cost-effective, biodegradable alternative to expensive hanging baskets.

Key advantages

  • 100% biodegradable, turning into organic matter on decomposing
  • Holds more water for faster cultivation and shock-free re-establishment
  • Good root protection with no deformity in the root system
  • Roots penetrate the pot walls; air contact naturally prunes aerial roots
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor plants, including hydroponics
  • Basket liners hold moisture while allowing drainage and air circulation
  • Display plants on walls, under windowsills or on deck railings
