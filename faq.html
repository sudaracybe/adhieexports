Frequently Asked Questions

What products do you export?

Coco grow cubes, coco grow poles, cocopeat grow discs and pellets, coir pots and basket liners, and coir weed control mats.

Can you make products to our specification or under our brand?

Yes. We offer OEM, ODM and private-label manufacturing, and size, packing, thickness and moisture/EC levels can be customised to your requirements.

What are the moisture and EC levels?

Our cubes, discs and pellets have moisture of less than 20%. EC level is set for seed germination (cubes) or as per customer specification (discs and pellets).

What packing options are available?

Naked, cling-wrapped, shrink-wrapped, netted or cartoned, depending on the product. Coco poles are packed 25 pieces per bundle.

What are your minimum order quantity, prices and lead time?

These depend on the product, size, packing and order volume. Send us your requirements and we will prepare a tailored quotation. We typically reply within 1-2 business days.

Can we get samples?

Yes, contact us for samples. Tell us the product, size and packing you need; sample availability and courier arrangements are confirmed individually.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Export shipping is arranged from Sri Lanka.

Do you supply throughout the year?

Yes, we offer consistent supply for all farming seasons.

Where are you located?

Kurunegala, North Western Province, Sri Lanka, in the heart of the country's coconut-growing region.

Still have a question? Send us your requirements and we will reply within 1-2 business days.

Send us your product, size and packing needs for a quotation.
