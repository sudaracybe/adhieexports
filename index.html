Adhie Exports & Trading (Private) Limited is a Sri Lanka-based manufacturer and exporter of coconut coir and coco peat products. Based in Kurunegala, in the heart of Sri Lanka's coconut-growing region, we process natural coconut husk into high-quality, 100% organic growing media and garden products for nurseries, commercial growers, hydroponic farms and home gardeners around the world.

Coco Grow Cubes

Coco Grow Cubes

Premium cocopeat seedling cubes for plant nurseries

Adhie Coco Grow Cubes are crafted from premium cocopeat, an exceptional seedling base for root development. Designed primarily for plant nurseries, they significantly reduce the rejection rate of nursery plants. With independent control of water and fertilization during the seedling phase, the cubes minimise transplant risk, and they can be placed directly onto the growing media in later stages, saving time and money.

Coco Grow Poles

Coco Grow Poles

Natural coir support poles (moss sticks) for climbing plants

The Adhie Coco Pole (also known as a coir pole or moss stick) is a support pole for potted plant creepers, suitable for indoor and outdoor gardens. It is made by wrapping natural coir fibre, in mattress-fibre, rubberized coir pad or coir yarn form, around a hollow PVC or wooden core. Poles support vines, shrubs and other plants that need protection while growing, and are widely used in nurseries and horticulture farms.

Cocopeat Grow Discs & Pellets

Cocopeat Grow Discs & Pellets

Compressed coco peat discs and pellets for fast, clean germination

Adhie Coco Peat Discs are ideal for individual, quick seed germination and are widely used in home gardening and nurseries, especially Gerbera and Orchid nurseries. This quality compressed disc is specially treated for excellent results with potted plants and suits home and commercial hydroponics. Each disc fills a 1–2 litre pot when re-hydrated. We supply naked discs, individually shrink-wrapped units, or bulk shrink-wrapped packs.

Coir Pots & Basket Liners

Coir Pots & Basket Liners

Biodegradable coir pots and hanging-basket liners for shock-free transplanting

Adhie Coir Pots are made from coconut fibre from the outer layer of the husk, bonded with natural latex. Fully biodegradable, they are widely used in horticulture, ornamental, vine and tree nurseries and in domestic gardening. Their high permeability to water, air and roots gives faster cultivation, an excellent root system and re-establishment without transplant shock, even in cold, drought or adverse seasons. Pots come in different sizes, for manual or automated soil filling, and pair well with our Coco Discs.

Weed Control Mats

Weed Control Mats

Biodegradable coir mulch mats that protect young trees

Mulching around trees helps young trees get the nutrients they need in early development while suppressing weeds around the root system. In the first three years, young trees rely on moisture in the growing medium to carry minerals to the roots; weeds compete for that moisture and slow growth.
