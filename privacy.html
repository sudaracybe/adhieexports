Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Adhie Exports & Trading (Private) Limited ("we") handles information on adhieexports.com.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form we receive the details you enter: name, company, country, email, phone, product of interest, quantity, target price and message.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotations and arrange samples or orders. We do not sell your information.

Services we use

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Enquiries are delivered to us by Web3Forms. The contact page embeds a Google Map and pages load Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details under Google's own policies. We may use privacy-focused website analytics to count visits.

Your choices

To ask us to correct or delete information you sent us, email info@adhieexports.com.
