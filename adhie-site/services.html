Services: OEM, ODM & Private Label

Adhie Exports & Trading (Private) Limited supports buyers with flexible manufacturing and supply arrangements across our full coir and coco peat range:

  • OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturing): products manufactured to your specification
  • ODM (Original Design Manufacturing): products developed and packed to your design and brand
  • Private Label: packed and labelled under your own brand name
  • Custom sizing, thickness, moisture/EC levels and packaging for every product line
  • Flexible packing: naked, cling-wrapped, shrink-wrapped, netted or cartoned
  • Consistent supply for all farming seasons, with export shipping arranged from Sri Lanka
