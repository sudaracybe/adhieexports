Coco Grow Cubes

Premium cocopeat seedling cubes for plant nurseries

Coco Grow Cubes

Adhie Coco Grow Cubes are crafted from premium cocopeat, an exceptional seedling base for root development. Designed primarily for plant nurseries, they significantly reduce the rejection rate of nursery plants. With independent control of water and fertilization during the seedling phase, the cubes minimise transplant risk, and they can be placed directly onto the growing media in later stages, saving time and money.

The cubes are 100% organic and made from natural, sustainable cocopeat sourced and processed in Sri Lanka. Their porous structure improves nutrient absorption, and their high buffer capacity retains water that plants absorb as needed throughout the seedling stage.

Key advantages

  • Promotes rapid, dense root growth
  • Excellent growing control for precise nutrient absorption
  • 100% organic and environmentally friendly
  • Nutrients readily absorbed due to the porous structure
  • Reduces rejection rate of nursery plants
  • Ideal for plant nurseries and seedling development
  • Compatible with coco peat growing media
  • Minimizes risk and improves seedling success
