Cocopeat Grow Discs & Pellets

Compressed coco peat discs and pellets for fast, clean germination

Cocopeat Grow Discs & Pellets

Adhie Coco Peat Discs are ideal for individual, quick seed germination and are widely used in home gardening and nurseries, especially Gerbera and Orchid nurseries. This quality compressed disc is specially treated for excellent results with potted plants and suits home and commercial hydroponics. Each disc fills a 1–2 litre pot when re-hydrated. We supply naked discs, individually shrink-wrapped units, or bulk shrink-wrapped packs.

Adhie Coco Pellets are made from the same premium coco peat: 100% biodegradable, porous, and high in water retention, which promotes rapid root growth. Add water and the pellet expands to several times its size. As the plant develops, transfer it straight into the growth medium or a grow cube.

Key advantages

  • Facilitates quick, individual seed germination
  • Maintains loose, airy soil for better root growth
  • Excellent water absorption and retention, reducing watering frequency
  • Biodegradable and environmentally friendly
  • Convenient, mess-free alternative to traditional soil
  • Promotes successful transplanting with minimal shock
  • Suitable for home gardening, nurseries and commercial hydroponics
  • Chemical-free and safe for plants and the environment
