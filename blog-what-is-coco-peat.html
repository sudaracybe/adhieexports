What is coco peat and why do growers use it?

Coco peat (also called cocopeat) is the fine, spongy material that remains when coir fibre is separated from coconut husk. It holds water while keeping air around the roots, which is why nurseries, hydroponic farms and home gardeners use it as a growing medium.

It is a renewable by-product of the coconut industry and is biodegradable. For shipping it is usually compressed into blocks, discs or pellets that expand when water is added.

Quality depends on how the peat is processed, so buyers commonly ask about moisture, EC (electrical conductivity) and packing. Our cubes, discs and pellets are supplied with moisture below 20%, and EC can be set to your specification.
