About Us

Adhie Exports & Trading (Private) Limited is a Sri Lankan manufacturer and exporter specialising in coconut coir and coconut peat-based horticultural products. Operating from Kurunegala, in Sri Lanka's North Western Province, a region renowned for coconut cultivation, we transform coconut husk, a natural by-product of the coconut industry, into sustainable growing media and garden products used by nurseries, commercial growers and hydroponic farms worldwide.

Our range covers the full seedling-to-cultivation cycle: coco grow cubes and cocopeat discs/pellets for germination, coir pots and basket liners for transplanting, coco grow poles for supporting climbing plants, and weed control mats for young trees. Every product is 100% natural, biodegradable and produced with the environment in mind.

We offer OEM, ODM and private-label manufacturing, and all specifications (size, packing, thickness, moisture and EC levels) can be customised. Our goal is to be a dependable long-term supply partner for importers, distributors and growers around the world.
