Coco Grow Poles

Natural coir support poles (moss sticks) for climbing plants

Coco Grow Poles

The Adhie Coco Pole (also known as a coir pole or moss stick) is a support pole for potted plant creepers, suitable for indoor and outdoor gardens. It is made by wrapping natural coir fibre, in mattress-fibre, rubberized coir pad or coir yarn form, around a hollow PVC or wooden core. Poles support vines, shrubs and other plants that need protection while growing, and are widely used in nurseries and horticulture farms.

The pole's high moisture retention lets plant roots grip the natural organic coir mattress. Adhie Coco Poles are available in a range of diameters and lengths to suit any application.

Key advantages

  • Porous with high water-holding capacity, helping plants develop many tiny roots across the surface
  • Plants grow healthier when supported with coir poles
  • Easily installed and a reliable replacement for traditional moss sticks
  • Gives ideal support while maintaining a moist environment for roots
  • Well suited to creepers and ornamental plants indoors, on patios or in the garden
