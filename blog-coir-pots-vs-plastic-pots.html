Coir pots vs plastic pots for nurseries

Coir pots are made from coconut fibre from the outer layer of the husk, bonded with natural latex. Water, air and roots can pass through the walls, so roots grow into the pot instead of circling inside it.

Because the whole pot is biodegradable, plants can go into the ground with the pot, which helps avoid transplant shock and root disturbance. Pots are available in different sizes for manual or automated soil filling.

Coir pots can dry out faster than plastic, so watering needs a little more attention. Many nurseries pair them with coco discs as a ready-made potting medium.
