How to use coco peat discs and pellets

Place the disc or pellet in a pot or container and add water until it expands to several times its original size. A hydrated disc fills a 1-2 litre pot.

Let the peat absorb the water fully, then sow your seeds and keep the medium evenly moist. Pellets give each seed its own clean start, which suits herbs, vegetables and ornamental plants.

When the seedling has grown, a pellet can be moved straight into the growing medium or into a grow cube, which keeps transplant shock low.
