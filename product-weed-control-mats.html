Weed Control Mats

Biodegradable coir mulch mats that protect young trees

Weed Control Mats

Mulching around trees helps young trees get the nutrients they need in early development while suppressing weeds around the root system. In the first three years, young trees rely on moisture in the growing medium to carry minerals to the roots; weeds compete for that moisture and slow growth.

Adhie Weed Control Mats suppress weeds so moisture reaches the tree. They also stop the growing medium drying out and cracking, which speeds water loss, letting the full root system establish for fast, healthy growth.

Key advantages

  • 100% biodegradable, turning into organic matter on decomposing
  • Conserves water and insulates, moderating moisture around plants
  • Easily installed around trees; protects against weeds and soil erosion
  • Tough, flexible bonded mat that passes air, water and nutrients but blocks weeds and grass
  • Promotes seedling growth and reduces plant mortality
  • Improves soil health, productivity and erosion resistance
